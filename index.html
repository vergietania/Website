Overview

The project is called Rupa Rasa, where I will dive deeper into Indonesian traditional snacks.

The name Rupa means appearance or form and Rasa means taste. Together, they mean form and taste.

In this journal, I will explore and experiment with different outcomes to highlight that traditional snacks are the main star of the meal.
