Overview

The project is called Rupa Rasa where I will dive deeper about Indonesian traditional snacks. The name Rupa means appearance or form and Rasa means taste, together they mean form and taste. In this journal, I will explore and experiment different outcomes to highlight that traditional snacks are the main star of the meal.
